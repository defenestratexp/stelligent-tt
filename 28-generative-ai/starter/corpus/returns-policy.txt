Trailhead Outfitters - Returns and Exchanges Policy
(Fictional company. Training material for Stelligent-U Topic 28.)

Standard returns
Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of
delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Refunds are
issued within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.

Opened or used items
Opened items can be returned within 30 days for store credit only, unless
they are defective. Worn footwear cannot be returned unless it is defective.

Defective or damaged items
Items that arrive damaged, or that fail because of a manufacturing defect,
can be returned at any time within the warranty period for a replacement
or a full refund, at the customer's choice. See the Warranty document.

Exchanges
Size and colour exchanges are free within 30 days. We ship the replacement
as soon as the carrier scans the returned item.

Return shipping
Return shipping is free for defective items and exchanges. For other
returns, a flat 7.95 USD return-label fee is deducted from the refund.

Final sale
Items marked "final sale", gift cards and personalised items cannot be
returned.
