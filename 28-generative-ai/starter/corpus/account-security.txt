Trailhead Outfitters - Your Account and Security
(Fictional company. Training material for Stelligent-U Topic 28.)

Password resets
Reset links expire after 30 minutes. If the reset email does not arrive,
check spam and make sure the address on file is correct. Support staff can
never see or set your password.

Two-step verification
Turn it on under Account > Security. We support authenticator apps and
passkeys. SMS codes are no longer offered for new enrolments.

Suspicious orders
If an order you did not place appears on your account, reset your password
immediately and contact support. We cancel unshipped orders and investigate
shipped ones.

Deleting your account
Request deletion under Account > Privacy. We delete personal data within 30
days, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices.

What we will never ask for
Support will never ask for your password, your full card number or a
one-time code by email, chat or phone.
