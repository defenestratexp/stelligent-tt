Trailhead Outfitters - Shipping
(Fictional company. Training material for Stelligent-U Topic 28.)

Processing time
Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern time on a business day ship the same
day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

Options and prices (continental US)
Standard ground: free on orders over 75 USD, otherwise 6.95 USD; 3 to 7
business days.
Two-day: 14.95 USD.
Overnight: 29.95 USD; order by 11 a.m. Eastern time.

Upgrading after ordering
You can upgrade the shipping speed until the order status changes to
"packed". After that, contact support; we cannot guarantee an upgrade.

PO boxes and APO/FPO
We ship to PO boxes by standard ground only. APO/FPO addresses take 10 to
20 business days.

Lost or missing packages
If tracking shows "delivered" but you cannot find the package, wait 48
hours, check with neighbours, then contact support. We file the carrier
claim and send a replacement once the claim is opened.

Damaged in transit
Photograph the box and the item and contact support within 7 days. This is
handled as a shipping claim, not a return.
