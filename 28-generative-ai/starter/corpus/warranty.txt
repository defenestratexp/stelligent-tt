Trailhead Outfitters - Warranty
(Fictional company. Training material for Stelligent-U Topic 28.)

Coverage
Trailhead-brand products carry a 2-year warranty against defects in
materials and workmanship. Tents and backpacks carry a 5-year warranty.
Other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty.

What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, accidents, fire, UV degradation
after the first year, and alterations or repairs not made by us.

How to claim
Contact support with your order number and photos of the defect. We may
ask you to send the item in. Approved claims get a repair, a replacement
or a refund, in that order of preference.
